## II. The Four Core Assemblies of Automobiles (Universal for All Vehicles, Compatible with Fuel/Electric Vehicles)
### 1. Powertrain — the vehicle's power source
It is responsible for converting **chemical energy (gasoline/diesel) or electrical energy into rotational torque**, providing driving force for the entire vehicle.
- Core fuel vehicles: `engine, turbo, intercooler, fuel supply, exhaust system, ignition system`
- Hybrid/range extender added: `Generator and planetary gear coupling mechanism`
- Pure electric vehicle replacement: `Drive motor, power battery, motor reducer gear, high-voltage electronic control`
- **Key Highlights of Cold Door Parts in This Lesson**: `Turbine Floating Bearing, VVT Regulating Gear, Water-cooled Intercooler, Oil Pump Gear, Planetary Row Gear`

### 2. Chassis Assembly—Power Transmission + Driving Handling Load
It receives the torque output from the powertrain, controls vehicle movement, stopping, steering, and shock absorption, making it the most densely packed mechanical gear and valve assembly.
Includes four major subsystems:
- Transmission system: `Clutch, complete gear set for transmission, synchronizer gear ring, differential bevel gear, half shaft`
- Driving system: `wheels, hubs, suspension arms, various bushings, shock absorbers`
- Steering system: `Steering machine rack gear, steering tie rod`
- Braking system: `Master brake cylinder, sprint pump, vacuum booster valve, ABS valve body`
Marked cold door parts: `Differential gear, synchronizer gear bushing, steering gear, pressure relief check valve`

### 3. Body assembly — load-bearing, protection, cabin space
Divided into **load-bearing and non-load-bearing types**, it accommodates passengers, absorbs collision impacts, and installs all external accessories.
- Exterior: `Bumper, bumper beam, fender, doors, ABC pillars, spoiler`
- Interior: `Dashboard frame, seat adjustment rack, door lock gear, ceiling soundproofing structure`
> Niche small parts: `Door locking gears, chassis air guide spoilers, body rib connection plates`

### 4. Electrical and electronic assemblies — whole-vehicle control, power supply, sensing
It is divided into two main systems: the **low-voltage 12V system' and the new energy high-voltage system**, which are essentially the vehicle's **nerves and brain**.
- Low voltage: `battery, headlights, sensors, window lift motor, air conditioning control panel`
- High voltage (hybrid/pure electric): `VCU vehicle controller, MCU motor controller, BMS battery management, high-voltage distribution box`
> Precision Cold Door Components: `Various pressure sensors, vacuum solenoid valves, differential pressure sensors, high-voltage insulated fuses`

## III. Complete Working Logic of Three, Four Major Assemblies (One-Sentence Closed Loop)
>**powertrain outputs torque → chassis gear transmission mechanism transmits power to drive the wheels → The body carries people and equipment and provides protection → The electrical assembly collects real-time vehicle data, automatically adjusts power/cooling/braking, and operates in full coordination.**

## IV. Five Major Vehicle Drive Layouts (Structure, Advantages and Disadvantages, Applicable Models)
### 1. FF front-engine, front-wheel drive (mainstream for family sedans/SUVs)
**The engine and transmission are all located in the front engine bay, driving only the front wheels.**
- Advantages: Compact structure, large interior space, short transmission distance, low cost; `
- Disadvantages: `Sudden acceleration shifts the center of gravity backward, front wheel grip decreases, high-horsepower tends to slip, and handling ceiling is low.`
> Internal gear features: `transverse gearbox, compact size, no long drive shaft.`

### 2. FR front-engine, rear-wheel drive (luxury sedan, rear-wheel-drive sports car, rugged sedan)
**The engine is front-mounted, delivering power to the rear differential via the driveshaft to drive the rear wheels.**
- Advantages: `Balanced weight distribution between front and rear axles, good cornering control, high-horsepower starts without rushing to steer; `
- Disadvantages: `Driveshafts occupy interior floor space and are more costly.`
> core niche structure: `Long drive shaft, rear axle differential gear set. `

### 3. RR Rear-Engine, Rear-Wheel Drive (Supercar, Older Porsche 911)

**The engine is arranged at the rear to directly drive the rear wheels.**
- Advantages: `Large rear wheel load, excellent grip at startup`
- Disadvantages: `The front end is light, low-speed steering is prone to pushing the head, and the maintenance space is limited.`

### 4. 4WD/AWD four-wheel drive (off-road, high-performance SUV, performance car)

**Simultaneously driving four wheels: timed four-wheel drive, full-time four-wheel drive, and just-in-time four-wheel drive.**

- Advantages: `Strongest grip on rough roads and rain and snow surfaces`
- Disadvantages: `Multiple gear mechanisms, heavy weight, higher fuel consumption.`
> exclusive and niche parts: `transfer case gear, center differential multi-plate clutch, torque distribution planetary gear. `

### 5. Extended-range dedicated series layout (Li Auto, AITO, and other range-extended models)

***The engine is only connected to a generator to generate electricity and does not directly drive the wheels; the wheels are entirely driven by the motor.***
> exclusive components: `generator motor, motor reduction gear, large-capacity power battery.'`

## VI. Basic Distinctions of Component Grades (Laying the Groundwork for Later Brand Pros and Cons Courses)
>- Original parts: `Automakers supply OEM parts, install original parts, offer the highest compatibility, and the most expensive price; `
> - Disadvantages: `Very low modification potential, heat dissipation/strength design leans towards home durability, low performance ceiling.' `

> Accessories for top brands (MAHLE, Schaeffler, BorgWarner, Garrett): `Same models produced by OEMs, quality close to the original, high cost performance; Different brands have their own focuses; follow-up special courses will compare their strengths and weaknesses. `

> - Standard aftermarket parts: `Domestic small factory imitation, extremely low price; `
> - Disadvantages:` poor gear precision, thin intercooler cooling fins, poor sealing, prone to oil leakage, boost air leakage, and rapid wear. `
> - Racing modification parts (enlarged intercooler, reinforced turbo, forged gears): `Reinforced materials, maximized heat dissipation and strength; `
> - Disadvantages: `High resistance at low speeds, extremely high cost, some may lose daily fuel efficiency.`
